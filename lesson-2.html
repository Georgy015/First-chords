Lesson 2

Two new chords: E and Dm

With E and Dm you'll know eight chords, and something more useful than any single shape: how major and minor chords are built from each other. One finger is often all that separates them.

2.1

The new chords

Tap a card to see how to place your fingers, and press Play to hear it.

Numbers on the dots are fingers: 1 index, 2 middle, 3 ring, 4 pinky. ○ play the string open, ✕ don't play it.

2.2

Major and minor: one note apart

Major chords sound bright and settled. Minor chords sound darker, a little sad. On guitar, the difference is usually one note, and often one finger. Play each pair and listen for the change.

the note that changes

2.3

Practice task: change on the beat

The hard part of a new chord isn't holding it, it's getting to it in time. Pick a pair, strum once on every beat, and switch when the big letter changes. Your hand should already be moving on the last beat before the change.
