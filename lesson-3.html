Lesson 3 · Practice

Changing between two chords

Knowing a chord shape is one skill. Getting to it on time, in the middle of a song, is another, and it's the one that makes songs sound like songs. This practice chapter drills four chord pairs you'll meet again and again.

3.1

How to practice

Every pair is practiced in two stages. Don't skip ahead: the second stage only works once the first feels easy.

  1. Stage 1 · start here

    Downstrokes only

    ↓   ↓   ↓   ↓

    One downstroke on every beat, four on each chord. All your attention goes to the left hand: landing the new shape on beat one.

  2. Stage 2 · after Stage 1

    Down and up

    ↓↑ ↓↑ ↓↑ ↓↑

    Add an upstroke between the beats. Your strumming hand never stops, so the chord change has to happen while it keeps swinging.

The rule: stay on Stage 1 until you can change cleanly at 80 BPM for a full minute, with every string ringing. Tick the box on each pair when you get there, and Stage 2 unlocks. Start every new pair at 60 BPM.

3.2

Chord pairs

Each card plays the pair in a loop: four beats on the first chord, four on the second. The big letter shows the chord you should be holding; the underlined slot is your last chance to move before the change.
